# Northwind Supply Co. - Pricing

All prices are in US dollars (USD) and exclude sales tax.

## Business account plans

A Northwind Business account gives your team wholesale discounts on every order, net-30 invoicing and priority support.

| Plan | Price | Seats | Discount on orders | What is included |
|------|-------|-------|--------------------|------------------|
| Starter | Free | 1 | 0% | Order tracking and saved addresses |
| Plus | $29 per month | Up to 5 | 8% | Free standard shipping on every order and priority email support |
| Business | $99 per month | Up to 25 | 15% | Net-30 invoicing, a dedicated account manager and a quarterly spend report |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Unlimited | Custom | Volume pricing, custom contracts, single sign-on (SSO) and a service-level agreement |

## Annual billing

Pay yearly and get two months free. Plus is $290 per year and Business is $990 per year. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your account page.

## Free trial

The Plus and Business plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start. If you do not upgrade, your account drops back to the free Starter plan.

## Volume pricing and quotes

Orders of 100 units or more of the same product qualify for volume pricing, typically 10% to 25% off list price depending on quantity. Volume pricing is quoted by our sales team: ask for a quote and we reply within one business day. Enterprise pricing is also by quote only.

## Cancelling or changing plans

You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel your Plus or Business plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period and are not refunded for unused time, except within 14 days of your first paid charge.
