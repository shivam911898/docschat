# Northwind Supply Co. - Shipping and Returns

## Shipping rates and delivery times

We ship from warehouses in Columbus, Ohio and Reno, Nevada. Orders placed before 2:00 pm Eastern on a business day usually leave the warehouse the same day; otherwise they ship the next business day.

| Method | Delivery time | Price |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Standard | 5 to 7 business days | $5.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Expedited | 2 to 3 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day (order by 2:00 pm Eastern) | $29.95 |

Plus and Business plan members get free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, Washington DC and US territories, and to Canada. Canadian orders ship by Standard International for $18.95 and take 7 to 12 business days; duties and taxes are collected at delivery. We do not currently ship to any other countries (for example the UK, Germany, France or Australia).

## Tracking your order

You receive a confirmation email when you order and a tracking link as soon as your order ships. You can also track any order under Orders in your account, or by entering your order number and email on the Track Order page.

## Return policy

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they are unused and in the original packaging. Start a return from the Orders page in your account to get a prepaid return label.

- Return shipping is free if the item is defective, damaged or we sent the wrong item.
- For any other reason, a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund.
- Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.
- Exchanges are free: we ship the replacement as soon as the return scans in transit.

## Items that cannot be returned

Custom-engraved products, opened consumables such as fuel and batteries, and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, email a photo to support within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement or refund at no cost to you, and you do not need to send the damaged item back.
