# Northwind Supply Co. - Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I contact customer support?

Email support@northwindsupply.example or call 1-800-555-0142. Phone support is open Monday to Friday 8:00 am to 8:00 pm Eastern and Saturday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm Eastern. Email is answered within one business day.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal and Apple Pay. Business plan customers can also pay by invoice on net-30 terms after a short credit check.

### Do you charge sales tax?

Yes. Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address. Tax-exempt organizations can upload a valid exemption certificate under Account, then Tax settings.

### Can I change or cancel an order?

You can change or cancel an order within one hour of placing it from the Orders page. After that the order goes to the warehouse and cannot be changed, but you can still return it under our 30-day return policy.

### Is there a discount for students, teachers or nonprofits?

Yes. Verified students, teachers and registered nonprofits get 10% off every order. Verify with a .edu address or your organization's documents under Account, then Discounts.

### Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Digital gift cards come in $25, $50, $100 and $250 amounts and are delivered by email within minutes. Gift cards never expire and cannot be returned or refunded.

### What warranty do your products have?

Every Northwind product has a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Tents and backpacks are covered for 5 years. The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse or damage from accidents. Contact support with your order number to make a claim.

### Do you offer bulk or wholesale discounts?

Yes. Orders of 100 or more units of one product get volume pricing. Business and Enterprise plan members also receive standing discounts of 15% or more. Ask for a quote and our sales team will reply within one business day.

### How can I get a demo of the Business account?

Our team offers a free 20-minute walkthrough of Northwind Business by video call. Ask for a demo and leave your name and email and a team member will contact you within one business day to schedule it.

### Do you share my personal data?

No. We never sell your personal data. We only share what is needed with our payment processor and shipping carriers to fulfil your order. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support.

### Where is my order?

Check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email, or open Orders in your account. Standard orders usually arrive in 5 to 7 business days. If tracking has not moved for 5 business days, email support and we will open a carrier investigation.
