## FAQ (5 questions)

**1. What kinds of documents can the chatbot learn from?**
PDF, plain text and Markdown files with a real text layer, for example FAQs, price lists, shipping and returns policies, product catalogues, onboarding guides and manuals. Scanned image-only PDFs need to be converted to text first. For website content, send me the page URLs and I turn them into documents (included in Standard and Premium).

**2. Will the bot make things up?**
It is built not to. It is instructed to answer only from the passages retrieved from your documents, and when the answer is not there it says so and points the visitor to your contact details or offers to take their details. Each answer shows its source file, so you can check it. No chatbot is perfect, so I recommend testing it with your real customer questions before launch, and I help you do that.

**3. Do I have to pay for the AI? What about hosting?**
The app itself has no subscription. The AI runs on your own AI key (Groq or Gemini). Groq's free tier covers small traffic, and paid usage is typically a few dollars a month. Hosting (Vercel) and the database (Turso) have free tiers. I set everything up in your own accounts so you stay in control.

**4. Which website platforms does the widget work on?**
Any platform that lets you add a script tag: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, custom HTML and React sites. You paste one line of code and a chat bubble appears. If you cannot add code, you can also share the full-page chat link.

**5. Who owns the code and my data?**
You do. You receive the full source code under the MIT licence, the documents and leads live in your own database and accounts, and I keep no copy of your data after delivery.
